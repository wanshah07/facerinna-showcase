Games on the booth page

Untick a game to take it off the booth page: off the wheel, off the ranking board and out of the chat. Anyone who opens it from an old link or a QR code sees a “resting” card with the way back, and it gives no gift while it is off. Tick it again to bring it back. Then press Save settings.

The booth script running now is from before game switches, so it would not keep this. Paste the new booth-admin.gs into the script and deploy, then reload this page.

The gift at the end of a game

A game ends on a QR code once it scores enough. Scan it with the counter phone and the wheel picks the product. One gift per device, whichever game earned it. Leave it off on a day nobody is at the counter to scan.

The games are scored on scales with nothing to do with each other, so each has its own figure. Leave one empty and that game offers no gift — the safe way round, since a figure set too low hands one to everybody. Watch an hour of real scores on the booth board, then fill these in. UV Card is not here: it deals a random number rather than scoring a run, and hands out its own prize already.

The products on the wheel, and how many are left, are under 🎁 Gifts.

What this does

Locked and Hidden draw a screen over the booth page; they do not take the file off the server, so treat them as a closed door, not a safe. The reports vault is gated by its own script and is not affected.

Changes reach the page on its next load. A phone that is offline keeps showing what it last saw.

Games, the events page and the vault are not covered by the lock; only the booth home page is.

Each part of the page

Show is the ordinary state. Locked draws a passcode card in that section's place. Hidden takes the section, its divider and its menu links off the page.

A locked section opens with its own passcode if you give it one, otherwise with the site passcode from the Settings tab. Leave the passcode empty to use the site one.

As with the whole-page lock, a locked section's own markup is still in the published file, so this is a closed door and not a safe. Use it to keep a part of the booth back until you want it seen.

＋ Add a product

Each gift handed out takes one off its product. A product at 0 leaves the wheel by itself and comes back as soon as you restock it. When every product is at 0 the games stop giving out new codes.

Add restocks: it adds to what is left, so gifts handed out while you were typing are not undone. Set is for correcting a count after counting the boxes.

Letting a device play again

One gift per device. To let a visitor's phone earn another, find them under Claims and tap Let play again; their next good run gives them a new code.

The old row stays, marked reset. A code that phone had not spent yet stops working, so the old QR and a new one cannot be two gifts.

How it works

An admin has every area and can change who has access. A staff member has only the areas ticked for them; tap an area on their card to tick or untick it. A change takes effect on that person's next action, on every device they are signed in on.

Nobody can change their own access here, and the owner (the account the booth script runs as) always has everything. Each change records who made it and when, in the updated and by columns of the Admins tab in the booth sheet.
