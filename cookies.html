Cookie settings

What this site keeps in your browser, and the button that clears it. Last reviewed 20 September 2026.

This site sets no cookies. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixel and nothing from a third party following you between sites. That is why there is no banner asking you to accept anything — there is nothing to accept.

What it does keep, on this device only

A few things are stored in your own browser so the pages behave sensibly. None of it is sent anywhere by the act of browsing, and none of it identifies you by name unless you typed your name into a game yourself.

WhatWhyHow long
The name you typed into a gameSo the leaderboard can show your runs as yoursUntil cleared
A random id for this deviceSo a gift is given once per device rather than once per gameUntil cleared
Your background and display choicesSo the page looks the way you left itUntil cleared
Sections you have unlocked with a passcodeSo you are not asked again on every visitUntil cleared
A counter sign-in, on staff devices onlySo the booth phone is not signed in per visitor30 days
An offline copy of the pagesSo the site still opens when the venue's wifi does notUntil cleared or replaced

Clear it

This removes everything above from this browser, including the offline copy. Nothing on our side is affected. If this is a booth staff phone, it will sign out and need its code again.

Clearing it in the browser instead

Every browser can do the same from its own settings — usually under privacy, then site data for my.facerinna.com. Turning on private or incognito browsing keeps nothing after you close the window.

The rest

What we collect when you actually fill something in — an event registration, a game score — is set out in the privacy notice. The terms for using the site are on the terms of service page.
