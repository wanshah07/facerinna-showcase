What is collected, and when

Nothing here is collected by browsing. Each row happens only when you fill something in or finish a game.

WhenWhat is recordedWhere it goes

Kept on your device only

These never leave the browser you are using and are not readable by us: the booth page's saved copy of its own settings (so it still opens with no signal), which sections you have unlocked during this visit, and the scores played on that device. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of them.

What is not collected

There is no advertising or analytics tracking on these pages, no third-party cookies, and no profiling. Photographs of the brochures and products on the site are our own material, not anything taken from you.

Why

  • Event registration — to expect you, to prepare enough places, and to reach you if the session moves or is cancelled.
  • Vault access — the test reports are for healthcare professionals, so we need to know who is asking before we open them, and to be able to close them again.
  • Leaderboard — to show a ranking at the booth and to hand out the right reward. You choose the name; it does not have to be your full one.
  • Admin sign-in — so that only the people we listed can change the page.

Who else sees it

Google, as the operator of Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail, which is where the data is stored and from which our mail is sent. The pages themselves are served by GitHub Pages, which receives the ordinary request information any web server does. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

How long it is kept

An approval link for the vault expires 14 days after it is issued, and an admin session 30 days after sign-in, whether or not the underlying record is still held.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you may ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, stop using it for a purpose, or delete it. Write to the contact address above and say which you want; we will answer in writing. There is no charge for a first request.

Withdrawing consent is enough on its own: for the leaderboard, ask and we will remove the row. For vault access, say so and the link is revoked the same day.

Security, honestly stated

The workbooks are private to our Google account and the scripts read them as us, so the pages themselves carry no keys. A vault link, however, is a key: whoever holds it can open the reports until it expires or we revoke it, so please do not forward yours.

The booth page's lock and hide are a closed door, not a safe. They keep parts of the page out of sight between events; they are not a protection for anybody's personal data, and no personal data is stored in the page itself.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes to what is collected will be told to you where you give it.
