How to read the bars. Facerinna figures are mean % change vs baseline from third-party instrumented reports (Courage+Khazaka MPA580, Antera 3D, VISIA, PRIMOS-CR), single-arm before/after, n ≈ 30–32, sensitive-skin-screened panels, p < 0.05 unless flagged. Competitor figures are the strongest number that brand puts in the public record. A larger % from a single-arm study does not beat a smaller % from a randomised controlled trial.
