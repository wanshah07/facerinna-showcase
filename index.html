Baseline Day 14

After 14 days — visibly reduced redness. Under-eye wrinkle mapping showed the same trend.

SGS patch tests · 0% irritation

Single-application 24h occlusion patch tests on sensitive skin — 330/330 subjects across the Niacinamide, Retinol and Salicylic ranges with zero irritation at 0.5h, 24h and 48h (COLIPA 1997, Declaration of Helsinki).

In-vivo SPF 63.8 · ISO 24444

Serum Sunscreen achieved mean SPF 63.8 vs the 50 target — double-blind, dermatologist-supervised, CI < 17% (Skinproof, report SPF.PDD.R.590).

COLIPA 48H · B5 barrier range

All three B5 barrier creams: Grade 0 (no reaction) in 31 sensitive-skin subjects at every timepoint, under dermatological supervision (Bureau Veritas).

28-day efficacy · P < 0.01

Instrumented 28-day studies across the Niacinamide, Retinol, Salicylic and Ceramide/B5 ranges: hydration, oil, acne, melanin, wrinkles and barrier — every endpoint statistically significant (P<0.01 / P<0.001).
